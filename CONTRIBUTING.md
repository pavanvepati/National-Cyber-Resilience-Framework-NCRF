# Contributing to the National Cyber Resilience Framework (NCRF)

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the National Cyber Resilience Framework (NCRF)! This document outlines the guidelines for contributions related to ethical hacking, bug bounty programs, white-hat hacker contributions, vulnerability reporting procedures, responsible disclosure, and reward mechanisms.

## Ethical Hacking
- We encourage ethical hacking practices. Please ensure that all testing activities are conducted lawfully and ethically.
- Only test systems for which you have explicit permission.

## Bug Bounty Programs
- Our bug bounty program rewards individuals for reporting vulnerabilities. Contributions should be made through the bug bounty platform specified on our website.
- Report vulnerabilities to the designated program manager with detailed information.

## White-Hat Hacker Contributions
- White-hat hackers are encouraged to contribute by identifying security weaknesses and suggesting improvements.
- Contributions can include code submissions, documentation improvements, and security audits.

## Vulnerability Reporting Procedures
- If you discover a vulnerability, please report it through our official channels. Include steps to reproduce the issue, impact assessment, and any relevant logs.
- Please refrain from publicly disclosing vulnerabilities until they have been addressed.

## Responsible Disclosure
- We follow a responsible disclosure policy. This means that you should wait for us to address the vulnerability before sharing it publicly.
- We will communicate with you throughout the resolution process and notify you when it is safe to disclose.

## Reward Mechanisms
- Depending on the severity and impact of the reported vulnerabilities, rewards may be offered in the form of public recognition or monetary incentives as specified in our bug bounty program.

Thank you for helping us make our framework secure! If you have any questions, please contact us directly.
